## Numbers that don't add up
- **Broughton:** a $57.3m premium on a $500m floor is 11.5%, not 9.8%. 9.8% implies a limit of about $585m. Separately, "the policy paid off" is uncertain, since the Virginia Court of Appeals reversed and remanded.
- **Steinitz, Follow the Money:** "24 of 94 district courts — 14 local rules, two standing orders, ten local forms" sums to 26.
- **Siegel, Big Law Cuts Back:** $2.8bn is probably the year to mid-2025, not mid-2024.
- **Burford, Case studies:** says "Patent & IP is four of the twelve," but its own table lists three.
- **Fast Trak:** the Facts say fees from 5–10 cases were pledged; the Rule and Reasoning say ~18 matters. Probably 5–10 per contract and ~18 in total.
