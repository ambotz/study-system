* Hiring model for litigation fund Omni Bridgeway:
	* In that we recruit lawyers from law firms, oftentimes defense firms, or even those who started at a defense firm and then were in-house for a while. In any event when we're hiring lawyers, they are always lawyers with at least 6, preferably more like 8, and preferably maybe even more years of actual litigation experience.
	* There are so many reasons for that beyond just knowing how to spot litigation risk and suss out whether a damage model is ridiculous: looking at budgets, looking at how cases are being managed by the law firms we're working with, and just having the experience to see all these things that require funded investment to be optimally successful as well.
