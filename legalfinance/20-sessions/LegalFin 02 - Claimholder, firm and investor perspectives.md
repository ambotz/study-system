How do contingency firms determine what to charge clients?

Does a marketplace exist for litigation claims? No
* Very hard to standardize litigation funding --> no clean market and repeatable arbitrage opportunities (especially in commercial litigation). The underwriting is not a science. Exception: mass torts


Insurance in Underwriting

Judgement preservation insurance (JPI)

 Ex. Win a $100 million verdict. Now it's up on appeal. You could go to insurance companies and say, "We would like to insure that verdict. We will pay you 10% of the verdict, so $10 million, and you will insure that we will get paid that verdict. If we lose the appeal you will write us an insurance company check for $100 million. If we win you won't pay anything and we will pay you."

This was an enormous market from 2020 to 2023 and then it completely blew up because they wildly mispriced the market. Now it barely exists. Insurance companies completely left what they call the contingent insurance market because they so underpriced the risk of appeals.
