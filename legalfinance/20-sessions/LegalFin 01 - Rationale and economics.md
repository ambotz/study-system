Deal example

- Funder puts in $3M

- Funder return: greater of 3.0x deployed capital or 30% of gross proceeds
- Waterfall: gross proceeds --> funder --> Claimaint
- Non-recourse: if the proceeds are $0, the claimaint owes nothing

I've put together four different outcomes here, just to start illustrating some of this:

1. A loss trial, meaning that there are zero proceeds --> funder gets $0, claimant gets $0, Funder MOC, claimant share $0
2. An early low settlement of $5 million --> funder gets $5M, claimant gets $0, Funder MOIC: 1.67x ($5M/$3M), claimant share: 0%. Common for claimaints to not get any leftover $
3. A mid-range settlement of $15 million --> funder gets $9M, claimant gets $6M, Funder MOIC: 3 ($9/3M), Claimant share: 40%
4. A trial win, fully collected, of $40 million models --> funder gets $12M, claimant gets $28M, funder MOIC is 4 ($12/3M), claimant share: 70%
