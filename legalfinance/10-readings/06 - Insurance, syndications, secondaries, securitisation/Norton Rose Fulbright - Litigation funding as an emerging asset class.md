## Seminar hooks

Questions to carry into the episode, each built on something already in the vault:

- **What is the collateral, exactly?** A securitisation needs a pool with estimable cash
  flows. Litigation positions are binary, long-dated, and — per
  [[Slingshot Capital - Secondary investing in litigation finance]] — get **worse as the pool
  seasons**, because the early resolutions leave and the tail remains. Any securitisation of a
  seasoned pool inherits that adverse selection. Listen for whether they address it.
- **How is duration handled?** Cases run 30 months at the multiple-maximising point and far
  longer in the tail. Noteholders want scheduled amortisation. Listen for how the structure
  bridges that — reserve accounts, a revolving period, insurance wraps
  ([[Litigation risk insurance]]), or simply a long legal final maturity.
- **What happens to privilege and confidentiality in a rated deal?** Rating agencies and
  investors need case-level diligence; funding agreements and diligence memoranda are the
  documents [[Burford - Legal Finance 101]] claims are work-product protected. Disclosing to
  an arranger, a rating agency and investors is a much wider circle than a single funder.
  This is the tension Class 8 is about, arriving early.
- **Does champerty travel to the noteholders?** If a jurisdiction's doctrine reaches whoever
  shares in proceeds, a securitisation multiplies the number of strangers sharing. See
  [[Champerty and maintenance]].
