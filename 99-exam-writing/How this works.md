# How this works

Three loops. Everything else in this vault is plumbing for them.

## 1. Review — daily, 20 min, no tooling

Open the attack outline in `<class>/50-outline/`. Read one gate. Close the file.
Say the test out loud. Open it again and check.

That is the whole review mechanism. It needs no queue, no cards, no artifact.

## 2. Reps — three or four times a week, 60–90 min

Open the next file in `<class>/60-drills/raw/`. The prompt and the budget are
already in it. Set a timer. Write. Stop at the buzzer and do not tidy.

Then say: **"grade my reps."**

I score each one, write the graded attempt to `<class>/60-drills/attempts/`, and
append a row to `error-log.md`. You read one row. Open
`99-exam-writing/Rep tracker.md` when you want the trend.

## 3. Capture — after each class, 20 min

Type your notes into the session file in `<class>/20-sessions/`. Signals while
it is fresh.

Then say: **"reconcile today's class."**

I write the Professor gloss into the modules it touched and revise
`professor_emphasis` and `exam_likelihood`. You read the glosses in Obsidian
when you feel like it.

---

## The three sentences

- *"grade my reps"*
- *"reconcile today's class"*
- *"I need rep files for X"*

Nothing else is required of you.

## What is mine

Reconciling, grading, generating rep files, keeping the tracker working.

## What is yours alone

`confidence` on a doctrine module, 1–5. Set it when you have reviewed the
module, as a by-product of outlining — not as a separate exercise. No agent may
write it.

## Closed

These were considered and dropped. They are not pending work.

- Triage drill — allocation is arithmetic off the cover page, not a skill
- Drill-queue script — the outline is the queue
- Flashcard artifact — review loop covers it
- Audio — optional, not part of the loop
- Master outline — `30-doctrine` already is one
- Standalone confidence pass — folded into review
