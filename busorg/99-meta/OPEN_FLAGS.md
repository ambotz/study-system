## Substance worth a second look
- **Corwin:** the file says a fully informed vote leaves waste *and bad faith* claims open. Singh v. Attenborough (2016) suggests only waste survives.
- **Special litigation committees:** the Zapata quote "independence, good faith, and reasonableness" follows the B&C note. The opinion itself may read "good faith and a reasonable investigation."
- **Poison pill:** "voting is not on that list" is true of § 13(d)(3), but Rule 13d-5(b) includes voting in the definition of a group.
- **Director primacy:** it says § 122(18) gives only contractual recourse, "not specific performance." The statute points to contract remedies "including for any failure to perform," which can include specific performance.
- **Segway:** the notes suggest Segway chose an oversight theory to avoid exculpation. § 102(b)(7) doesn't protect officers sued by the corporation anyway.
- **Shlensky:** the Rule section says "process failure that borders on fraud…". The same file's Context says the rule "protects substance, not process."
- **Cheff / Unocal:** the "inherent danger in the purchase of shares with corporate funds" line likely originates in Bennett v. Propp (1962), which Cheff quotes.
- **Coster / Blasius:** the case pack's text of Coster reads "reasonableness and proportionately" (sic). The module now quotes it that way.
