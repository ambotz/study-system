## Notes

Professor: "What counts as legislative vs. executive power" 
- INS (law passed) -- Chada must leave 
- IJ/AH (executive branch) -- Stay --> executive power that is valid
- House Veto (legislative branch) -- Chadha must leave --> interpreted as legislative power and ruled unconstitutional 

*But* the House is functionally doing the same thing as the AG. So what's the difference between legislative and executive power? It is the **PROCESS** of how that decision was reached.
* Legislative power = procedures involving bicameralism and presentment (according to Art. 1 §5)
* Executive power = Under Art. 2, more broad, something that is not the domain of legislative power

When asking if something is legislative or executive power, focus on how a branch got to a certain decision it made. 

Bill of attainder—Congress cannot pass a Bill of Attainder (individual judgement on an individual)
* For negative, criminal actions*
* Positive, targeted, civil actions aimed at certain people = Constitutionally valid for Congress to pass.
